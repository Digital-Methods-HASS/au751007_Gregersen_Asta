Thoughts before starting the analysis

As has been outlined in the written part of this assignment, the research question at the center of this assignment is whether absolutism had a positive effect on the duration of reign for the absolute monarchs of Denmark and Sweden. My initial thought is that because absolutism clearly defined the power of the monarchs, and put a stop to struggles over the throne, this led to longer reigns. The monarchs lived longer, and were thus able to reign longer. Absolutism was in effect in Denmark for almost 200 years, so it is supremely relevant to focus on the Danish monarchy when exploring the effects of absolutism.
Absolutism in Sweden is very interesting. Unlike other European nations, where absolutism was in effect for very long. Absolutism in Sweden only lasted for 47 years. The two Swedish absolute monarchs did reign for very long. However, this does not necessarily mean that absolutism had a positive effect on their duration of reign. I suspect that the average duration of reign will be higher during both Danish and Swedish absolutism. This is relevant, but it is not enough to answer the research question. Different plots will be created, which will then be explored in depth in the written part of the assignment, Then one is able to better discern whether absolutism did have a positive effect on the reigns of the Danish and Swedish monarchs.

Working with the data

The hypothesis at the center of this project, is that monarchs, whether Danish or Swedish, on average reigned longer during absolutism. The average duration of reign overall, before and during absolutism will be found. Then, the different averages can be compared.

Creating a dataframe containing only the Danish monarchs that ruled during absolutism

After having conducted research about the Danish monarchs that reigned during absolutism, I know that the monarch who introduced absolutism was Frederik the 3rd, whose reign began in 1648. The last king to rule during absolutism was Christian the 8th. His death in 1848 marked the end of absolutism in Denmark.

The filter() function is used to create the new data frame. Using first_year_ruled_denmark>1647, any monarch who reigned before 1647 is excluded, as Frederik the 3rd ascended the throne in 1648. The average duration of reign both before and during absolutism was also calculated, and it was 18,1 years. This is relevant in relation to the plots that have been created. The average duration of reign before absolutism was 11,3 years, the average duration of reign during absolutism was 29 years and the overall average was 13,1 years. The average duration of reign both before and during absolutism was also calculated This was 11,8 years, and is relevant in relation to the different figures. If one only considered the different averages, one could be inclined to conclude that absolutism did have a positive effect on the reigns of the absolute monarchs. One must consider the averages in relation to the monarchs before and during absolutism, before one can begin to answer the research question.
